Welcome to Telly Fixtures

An app for sports fans, from sports fans.

What is it? Simple: the biggest sports fixtures you can watch live on UK TV.

No ads. No betting! That's right, just your favourite sports and where to watch them.

And a buzzer to remind you about those games about to start, so that you don't miss a thing.

What it does

  • Today and the next 7 days, all in UK times.
  • My TV. Tick the competitions you follow and see just those, day by day.
  • Reminders. A bell on each fixture, so your phone tells you before it starts, even with the app closed.
  • Works offline. The last listings stay on your phone.
  • No sign-in, no ads, no betting and no tracking.
