What the app is

Telly Fixtures lists sport on UK TV. It doesn't show the sport itself, and to watch you'll need your own access to the channel or streaming service. It's for your personal use, and it isn't connected with any broadcaster, league or competition.

Listings can change: check with the broadcaster

Fixtures, times and channels come from other organisations and can change at short notice, sometimes after our last update. We work hard to keep them right, but we can't promise that every listing is correct or complete. If it matters, check with the broadcaster, especially before paying for a channel or making plans around a game.

The free tier
