What it does

  • Today and the next 7 days, all in UK times.
  • Channels you can trust. A channel appears only once a broadcaster's guide or the competition itself confirms it. Until then the app says "TV channel to be confirmed", not a guess.
  • My TV. Tick the competitions you follow and see just those, day by day.
  • Reminders. A bell on each fixture, so your phone tells you before it starts, even with the app closed.
  • Works offline. The last listings stay on your phone.
  • No sign-in, no ads, no betting and no tracking.
