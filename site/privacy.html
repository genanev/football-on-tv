Privacy policy

Draft, awaiting legal review This policy describes the app as it will be at launch, including purchases and promo codes. It hasn't had its final legal check yet. Draft of 5 November 2026.

In short

  • No ads, no tracking and no sign-in. We don't ask for your name or email to use the app.
  • Your choices are saved on your phone, not with us.
  • Google and RevenueCat handle purchases. We never see your card details.
  • We only hold something that could relate to you if you buy a subscription, use a promo code, send feedback or email us.
  • We never sell your data.

What's stored on your phone

The app saves these on your phone so it can work, including offline. They aren't sent to us.

  • Your picks: the competitions you've ticked for My TV.
  • Your settings: colour theme and how long before a fixture a reminder comes.
  • Your reminders: which fixtures, and when to alert you. Your phone fires them itself.
  • The date your free month started.
  • In the free tier, the sport you chose and the date you chose it.
  • The last listings downloaded, so the app works with no signal.
  • An anonymous ID, used only for purchases and promo codes (see below).

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this from the phone. If you've turned on Android's own backup, Android may include it in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. We can't see that backup.

The app asks for permission to send notifications only so that reminders can work. You can say no, or turn them off later in your phone's settings.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking tools in the app or on this website, and no selling or renting of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos or other files.
  • This website sets no cookies of its own.

What leaves your phone, and why

Downloading the listings

The app downloads the same listings file for everyone from our website address. Nothing about you is in that request, but like any website's host, Cloudflare sees your phone's internet (IP) address to deliver the file and protect against attacks. We don't use it to identify anyone. The same applies when you visit this website.

Buying a subscription

You pay through Google Play, using your Google account. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy, and your card details never reach us.

We use a company called RevenueCat to check that a subscription is real and still active, so that the app can unlock. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for this app (what was bought, when, and whether it's active), the anonymous ID and your phone's language setting and currency. It doesn't receive your card details. The app checks with RevenueCat while it's running, so RevenueCat also sees your IP address when it connects.

Google also gives us the records it gives every seller, such as the order number, item, price, date and buyer's country. If you ask for help with an order, we may look it up using the details you give us.

Using a promo code

When you enter a promo code, the app sends the code and the anonymous ID to our very small server, which runs on Cloudflare. The server checks the code, and asks RevenueCat to unlock the app for that anonymous ID for 3 months. We keep a record of the code and the anonymous ID, so that codes can't be used more often than they should be. The anonymous ID is a random code made for the app on your phone. It doesn't contain your name or email, or anything else about who you are.

Sending feedback (optional)

The Feedback button opens a form, run on Google Forms. Your answers are stored in a Google Sheet that we can read. The form is filled in beforehand with the app version, your type of phone and the screen you were on, and you can see these before you send it. Giving an email address is optional and only needed if you'd like a reply. Please don't include anything sensitive.

Emailing us

If you email us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply. Our email passes through Cloudflare and is stored in Google's Gmail.

Crash reports

If your phone is set to share usage and diagnostic data with Google, Google Play collects basic crash and performance data. We see this as totals and technical reports, to help fix problems. The app adds no crash reporting of its own.

Who else handles data

  • Google: Google Play purchases, crash data, the feedback form and email storage. Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: checking subscriptions and unlocking promo codes. RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosting the listings file and this website, the promo code server and forwarding email. Cloudflare's privacy policy.

These companies may handle data outside the UK, including in the United States, using the safeguards UK law requires for such transfers. We'd also share data if the law required it.

Why we're allowed to use this data

  • To provide what you've asked for (a contract): subscriptions, promo codes and replies to your messages.
  • Our legitimate interests: preventing misuse of promo codes, protecting the service from attacks, and improving the app using feedback and crash reports.
  • The law: keeping purchase records for tax.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Purchase records: for as long as your subscription is active, then for up to 6 years, for tax and accounting.
  • Promo code records: until 12 months after the code's 3 months have ended.
  • Feedback and emails: up to 12 months after we've dealt with them, or sooner if you ask.

Keeping it safe

Data sent by the app and this website travels over encrypted connections. The secret keys that unlock features are kept on the server, never in the app. Access to our accounts with these companies is limited to us and protected by two-step sign-in.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also object to how we use it or ask us to limit it. Email us and we'll reply within a month. To cancel a subscription, use Google Play (the terms explain how).

Because we don't know who you are, we may need something to find your data, such as your Google Play order number or the anonymous ID. We'll tell you how to find it.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

Telly Fixtures is a general-audience app and isn't aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its date. If a change matters to how your data is used, we'll also tell you in the app.
