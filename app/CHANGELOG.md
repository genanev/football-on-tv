# Telly Fixtures – what's new

One section per version uploaded to Google Play, newest first. The heading's number is the version in
`package.json`; the lines under it are the store's "What's new" text (500 characters at most), which
`npm run release` prints ready to paste.

## 1.0.6
- Reminders can now go off when a fixture starts, as well as an hour, 30 minutes or 15 minutes before.
- Reminders on time: allow Telly Fixtures to set alarms and reminders (from Settings or the Remind me screen), or your phone may deliver them a few minutes late.
- A reminder's bell no longer sits on a line of its own.

## 1.0.5
- Simpler reminders: tap a fixture and choose 15 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour before it starts.
- The big bells are gone: a small bell now shows only on the fixtures you've set a reminder for.

## 1.0.4
- Settings now has links to our Privacy policy and Terms of use.
- Small wording changes: reminders are now called "Fixtures reminders" in Settings, and clearer wording when a listing couldn't be checked on the last update.

## 1.0.3
- Match reminders: tap the bell on a fixture and your phone will remind you before it starts.
- New Settings button at the top right: choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes for reminders, and Light or Dark.

## 1.0.2
- The top bar now stays below the phone's status bar while you scroll.
- Specials only appear from a week before they start, and go once they're over.
- A shorter message when there's no internet connection.

## 1.0.1
- New app icon.

## 1.0.0
- First test version of Telly Fixtures: what's on UK TV for the next eight days, with UK times and channels.
- Football, motorsport, golf, cricket, rugby, darts, snooker, tennis, skiing and specials.
- My TV shows just the competitions you pick.
